

### 1. What is IaaS? (Infrastructure as a Service)
**IaaS** is the most basic layer of cloud computing. It allows you to **rent the raw hardware** (servers, storage, networks) over the internet instead of buying it.
*   **Analogy:** It is like renting an empty apartment. You get the walls and the floor (infrastructure), but you have to bring your own furniture (software) and clean it yourself (maintenance).
*   **You manage:** The Operating System (Windows/Linux) and everything installed on it.
*   **Provider manages:** The physical building (Data Center), electricity, and hardware repairs.
