### What are CAPEX and OPEX?
These are financial terms describing how businesses spend money.

*   **CAPEX (Capital Expenditure):**
    *   **Definition:** Spending money upfront to buy physical assets.
    *   **Example (On-Premise):** Spending $50,000 today to buy 5 servers. You own them, but you lose a lot of cash immediately.
*   **OPEX (Operational Expenditure):**
    *   **Definition:** Ongoing day-to-day expenses to run the business.
    *   **Example (Cloud):** Paying AWS $200 per month to rent those servers. You pay only for what you use, keeping your cash flow flexible.

**The Cloud Shift:** Cloud moves IT costs from **CAPEX** (buying heavy hardware) to **OPEX** (paying a monthly subscription).
