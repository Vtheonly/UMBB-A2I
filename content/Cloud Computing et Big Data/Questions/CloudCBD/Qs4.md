###  What is "Ubiquitous"?
In the context of the Cloud definition, **ubiquitous** means **"available everywhere, at any time."**

It means the service is not tied to a specific physical room. You can access your cloud resources (like Gmail or OneDrive) from:
*   Any **Location** (Home, Work, Algeria, France).
*   Any **Device** (Laptop, iPhone, Android Tablet).
*   Any **Time** (24/7).
